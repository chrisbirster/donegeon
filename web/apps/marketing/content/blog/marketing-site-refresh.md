# A clearer way to explore Donegeon

For a while, the product had more depth than the site around it. Donegeon already supported fast capture, recurring work, shared boards, team roles, onboarding, and calendar connections, but new visitors had to work too hard to understand that.

That gap created friction:

- New users do not understand how much of the product already exists.
- Teams evaluating Donegeon cannot easily evaluate the collaboration model.
- There is no durable place for docs, launch notes, or pricing context.

The update fixes that by making the product easier to evaluate and easier to trust.

## What changed

- Added dedicated features, docs, blog, and pricing routes
- Added clearer product and pricing pages
- Added guides and updates in one place
- Added plan-aware messaging for Personal, Pro, and Enterprise
- Expanded coverage of boards, quests, roles, and integrations

## Why this helps teams

Teams evaluating Donegeon can now see how the product works, how pricing changes as they grow, and where to go for help when they have questions.

## What comes next

The new structure also makes it straightforward to add:

- Feature walkthrough videos on selected docs
- Release notes or changelog-style posts
- Comparison pages and migration playbooks

That gives Donegeon more room to explain itself as the product keeps growing.
