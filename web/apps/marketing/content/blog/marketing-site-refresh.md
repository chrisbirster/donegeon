# A clearer home for teams using Donegeon

For a while, the product had more depth than the site around it. Donegeon already supported fast capture, recurring work, shared boards, team roles, onboarding, and calendar connections, but new visitors had to work too hard to understand that.

That mismatch creates friction:

- New users do not understand how much of the product already exists.
- Team buyers cannot easily evaluate the collaboration model.
- There is no durable place for docs, launch notes, or pricing context.

The refreshed site fixes that by making the product easier to evaluate and easier to trust.

## What changed

- Added dedicated features, docs, blog, and pricing routes
- Moved docs and blog into markdown-driven content flows
- Added plan-aware messaging for Personal, Pro, and Enterprise
- Expanded the feature narrative to cover the board, quests, roles, and integrations

## Why markdown matters

A product site usually needs frequent copy updates. Writing every guide or announcement as JSX slows that down and makes publishing feel heavier than it should.

Markdown changes that dynamic. Product updates can ship as content instead of route rewrites.

## What comes next

The new structure also makes it straightforward to add:

- Feature walkthrough videos on selected docs
- Release notes or changelog-style posts
- Comparison pages and migration playbooks

That is a much healthier foundation for a product that is still moving quickly.
